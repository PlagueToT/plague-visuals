About Me

THE CRAFT
BEHIND THE
CONTENT

I'm Plague — the video editor, designer, and motion artist behind Plague Visuals. I specialize in gaming and documentary-style content, built by working with real channels, real audiences, and real deadlines.

I've worked as a Virtual Assistant for RyanPictures — one of the most respected internet documentary channels — handling everything from editing to full channel operations. That experience sharpened me into someone who doesn't just cut footage, but manages the entire content pipeline.

My process is built around pacing. Every cut, every transition, every sound effect placed with intention. If you have footage that needs to become a video people actually finish watching — that's what I do.
